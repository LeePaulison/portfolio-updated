<ProjectHero title='AspAIre' subtitle='Your AI-powered career and job search assistant.'>
  <Overview>
    <strong>AspAIre</strong> reduces job-hunting fatigue by unifying job discovery, resume-aware matching, and
    AI-assisted application materials. It aggregates listings (Remotive + SerpAPI), parses your resume to surface
    relevant roles, suggests better search terms, and drafts tailored cover letters while supporting mock interview prep
    with streaming AI.
  </Overview>
</ProjectHero>

## My Role

- <strong>Architecture:</strong> designed layered backend with DAL/abstraction/business split
- <strong>GraphQL:</strong> authored schema/resolvers (users, preferences, resumes, job listings, AI settings, AI history)
- <strong>AI integration:</strong> summaries, cover letters, and interview chat (with streaming) wired to OpenAI
- <strong>Ingestion:</strong> job fetch + dedup pipeline; scheduling for API limits
- <strong>Frontend:</strong> Profile, Settings, Resume flows with shadcn/ui; accessibility-first patterns
- <strong>Ops:</strong> Vercel deploys, CI/CD via GitHub Actions, monitoring with Sentry

### Challenges

- <strong>API limits:</strong> query rotation and nightly runs to respect free-tier constraints
- <strong>Dedup logic:</strong> prioritizing canonical sources (e.g., Remotive) over overlaps
- <strong>Data split:</strong> relational (Postgres) vs. conversational/raw (MongoDB)
- <strong>Accessibility:</strong> ensuring forms, dialogs align with WCAG/508a

## Features

- Aggregated & deduplicated job feed from Remotive and SerpAPI
- Resume parsing to extract skills, titles, and industries for smarter matching
- AI-suggested search terms (alternative titles, related skills, locales)
- Tailored cover letter generation per job/company
- Interview prep via streaming AI with saved conversation history
- Application tracking across Saved → Applied → Interviewing → Offer
- Daily query sets and graceful handling of API limits
